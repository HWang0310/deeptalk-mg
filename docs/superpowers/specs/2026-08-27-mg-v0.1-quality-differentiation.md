# MG V0.1 Quality Differentiation

## Goal

Improve differentiation among causal, cycle, relationship, and abstract explanation scenes without changing the eight V0 benchmark definitions, renderer family, visual profile, or product boundary.

## Composition variation rules

`editorial-cn-v1` remains one design system. A grammar selects one of four intentional title/focal patterns: top-left thesis (`thesis`, `comparison`, `timeline`), left-rail causal (`causal`), centered loop (`cycle`), and structural field (`relationship`, `abstract`). No grammar may choose positions randomly.

## Meaning-bound grammar decisions

- **Causal:** a left-anchored cause launches a visible pressure pulse that travels through a directional spine. Later consequences receive progressively stronger emphasis, expressing transfer and accumulation.
- **Cycle:** stages sit on an open return path. The path visibly returns from adjustment to execution; a feedback marker signals reinforcement rather than a merely circular layout.
- **Relationship:** a central actor has stronger, thicker influence links to dependencies; peripheral actors are smaller and use lighter links. Node size and link weight encode relationship rather than count.
- **Abstract:** layers uncover a hidden supporting mechanism. A threshold line separates visible surface from accumulated underlying capacity, expressing compounding/constraint rather than decoration.

## Typography adversarial suite

Add independent `TypographyCase` fixtures for long Chinese judgment, multiline Chinese, narrow region, dense numeric values, percentage/unit, Chinese-English-number mixing, long label, and multi-layer hierarchy. The fitter must return all source characters or fail closed when a role cannot fit; it never clips or silently reduces below the readable role size.

## Evidence

Before replacing local render output, copy V0 contact sheets and QA JSON under `output/v0-baseline/`. Render V0.1 with the same benchmark ids and generate a `comparison.json` plus side-by-side contact sheets. Compare first-glance clarity, hierarchy, grammar differentiation, motion semantics, composition variety, template feeling, readability, and density through structured human review.

## Constraints

- Preserve all eight V0 benchmark inputs.
- Keep 1920×1080, 30fps, local and deterministic rendering.
- No DeepTalk Core change, unified Plugin Contract, Candidate Portfolio, real Episode integration, or new high-impact global profile.
